# EQUICAFI

## Equity Intelligence Engine

EQUICAFI is an educational equity-research platform that brings fundamental analysis, valuation, historical analysis, technical indicators, risk metrics, cross-metric reasoning, and hypothetical scenario modelling into one structured research workspace.

> **Research the business. Understand the numbers. Connect the signals.**

---

## Why EQUICAFI?

Financial analysis often means jumping between financial statements, ratios, charts, and different sources of information.

EQUICAFI brings these perspectives together into a single workflow so users can examine a company from multiple dimensions rather than relying on one metric.

Company
   ↓
Fundamentals
   ↓
Financial Structure
